Project Deck

One Acquisition. Two Data Systems. One Lakehouse.

An end-to-end ETL pipeline on Databricks Free Edition that consolidates a big FMCG retailer's data with a newly acquired smaller company — from raw S3 files to a governed, query-ready lakehouse.

Acquirer · structured, mature systems Acquired · inconsistent, unstructured data
01 · Business Problem

The merger created two truths, not one

Post-acquisition, leadership needs a single view of combined sales, customers and products — but the two companies' data doesn't speak the same language.

WHAT BROKE

→ Mismatched schemas between the two companies' source systems

→ Duplicate and conflicting customer & product identifiers

→ No shared reporting layer — each company reports in isolation

→ New transactions keep arriving; a one-time fix isn't enough

WHAT THE BUSINESS NEEDS

A pipeline that ingests both sources, reconciles them into a common model, and stays current automatically — so stakeholders can query the combined business the same day new data lands, in plain English if needed.

03 · Dataset Scope

Two very different data sources

Raw files from both companies land in Amazon S3 before anything is transformed.

ACQUIRER — STRUCTURED

  • Clean, consistently formatted CSVs
  • Stable customer & product IDs
  • Regular pricing & transaction feeds

ACQUIRED — UNSTRUCTURED

  • Inconsistent column naming & formats
  • Missing or conflicting keys
  • Manual exports with irregular cadence
